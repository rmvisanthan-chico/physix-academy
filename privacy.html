Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What we collect

PhysiX Academy has no accounts or sign-ups, and the app does not ask for personal information. Your progress, quiz results, and preferences stay in your browser. Aggregate analytics providers described below may process technical usage data when you accept analytics.

All progress, quiz results, and preferences are saved locally in your browser using localStorage. This local data does not leave your device unless you export it yourself.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics and Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (e.g., how many visitors the site gets). These tools collect anonymous usage data such as page views and browser type. They do not collect personal information or track individual users across websites.

Cookies

Analytics tools may set cookies for anonymous measurement purposes. No advertising cookies are used. You can block cookies in your browser settings without affecting the site's functionality.

Third-party services

The site loads KaTeX (for math rendering) and Google Fonts from public CDNs. If you accept analytics, Vercel and Google Analytics also receive technical usage data. These providers process information under their own privacy policies.

Local data

Progress, quiz results and preferences are stored locally in your browser. The site does not currently install a service worker, so loading the site or online-only simulations requires an internet connection.

Children's privacy

The platform is designed for students (typically ages 14 and above). We do not knowingly ask for or directly collect personal information. If a child uses the site, aggregate analytics providers may still process technical usage data when analytics is enabled.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
