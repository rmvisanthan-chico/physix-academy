Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

What we collect

PhysiX Academy runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no servers that store your information.

All progress, quiz results, and preferences are saved locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics and Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (e.g., how many visitors the site gets). These tools collect anonymous usage data such as page views and browser type. They do not collect personal information or track individual users across websites.

Cookies

Analytics tools may set cookies for anonymous measurement purposes. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. You can block cookies in your browser settings without affecting the site's functionality.

Third-party services

The site loads KaTeX (for math rendering) and Google Fonts from public CDNs. These requests are standard for any website and do not involve personal data transfer beyond your IP address, which is handled by those services according to their own privacy policies.

Offline data

PhysiX Academy can work offline via a service worker. The service worker caches site assets so pages load without an internet connection. It does not collect or transmit any data.

Children's privacy

The platform is designed for students (typically ages 14 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since the site does not collect any data at all, there is no risk of children's data being collected.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
