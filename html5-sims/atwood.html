Atwood Machine

a = (m2 − m1)g / (m1 + m2)

Two masses on one rope over a pulley. The heavier side always wins — but the acceleration stays small, because the tension pulling back is nearly as large as the weight driving it forward.

Both masses share one tension, so the tension is not m₁g. It is less than m₁g on the way up and less than m₂g on the way down — the difference is what accelerates each mass.
