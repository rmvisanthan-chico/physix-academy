Every half-wave is a pendulum of its own, swinging in step with its neighbours but in opposite sense. That is why the nodes never move and the string never carries energy away. The oscillation is drawn about 50× slower than real time, otherwise a 30 Hz string would just look like a blur. Readouts are true values. Drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom.
