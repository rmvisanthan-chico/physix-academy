# Projectile cannon — concept + parts (written before any geometry)

## One-sentence object
A bench cannon on a swivelling pivot that fires a brass ball on the true parabola x = v0 cosθ·t, z = v0 sinθ·t − ½ g t²; turning the ANGLE / POWER knobs re-aims it and the range dial previews R = v² sin2θ / g before you FIRE.

## Truth
- Physics: `projectileModel` in `model.mjs`, g = 9.81 m/s²; readout R, apex H = v² sin²θ / (2g), time of flight 2 v sinθ / g — all from the formula, never measured from the animation.
- Range: angle 10–80°, power 8–28 m/s, scaled to bench units (1 m = 3.5 bench units).
- Air resistance ignored, stated in the caption.

## Parts list (~48)
- Base: plate + halo, 4 feet (pad+bolt), top inset line, 4 corner screws, ruler strip + ticks, label plate w/ 3 rules.
- Cannon: pivot post + axle, trunnion discs, barrel body with muzzle ring + bore, breech block, counterweight, quadrant scale with ticks.
- Trajectory: trajectory dots pool (22), lit ball (flight pool), dust puff rings at muzzle (small).
- Target: arch frame + two posts, target board with ring marks, range ruler along ground, flag.
- Controls: FIRE pedestal + lit button cap; ANGLE knob; POWER knob (both knurled + pointers).
- Instrumentation: range dial (0–70 m), power ruler strip beside the breech.
- Wiring: cable pedestal → breech with connector shells.
