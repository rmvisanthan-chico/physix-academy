NCERT Physics, Class 9 to 12

The full NCERT physics sequence — every unit and chapter, explained with a simulation you can operate, a worked example, and a short check for understanding.

PhysiX Academy follows the NCERT order, so a student revising for a school test and a student preparing for boards see the same chapters in the same sequence. Each chapter pairs three things: a short explanation, an interactive simulation of the core phenomenon, and practice questions with worked solutions.

Class 9

Motion · Force and Laws of Motion · Gravitation · Work and Energy · Sound

Class 9 sets the vocabulary everything else uses — distance versus displacement, speed versus velocity, force as a vector, and energy as a quantity that is conserved. The simulations here are deliberately simple: a trolley, a falling ball, a vibrating string.

Class 10

Light — Reflection and Refraction · The Human Eye and the Colourful World · Electricity · Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

This is where ray diagrams and circuit reasoning start. The mirror and lens simulations let you drag the object and watch the image move, invert and change size — the same relationships the ray diagram rules describe, but live.

Class 11

Units and Measurements · Motion in a Straight Line · Motion in a Plane · Laws of Motion · Work, Energy and Power · System of Particles and Rotational Motion · Gravitation · Mechanical Properties of Solids · Mechanical Properties of Fluids · Thermal Properties of Matter · Thermodynamics · Kinetic Theory · Oscillations · Waves

Class 11 is the bridge from school physics to competitive physics. The same ideas return with calculus and vectors, so a student who only memorised Class 10 formulas tends to struggle here. The lessons are written to rebuild each idea from the ground up.

Class 12

Electric Charges and Fields · Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance · Current Electricity · Moving Charges and Magnetism · Magnetism and Matter · Electromagnetic Induction · Alternating Current · Electromagnetic Waves · Ray Optics · Wave Optics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter · Atoms · Nuclei · Semiconductor Electronics

The Class 12 simulations cover circuits, RC charging, magnetic fields, induction and optics — the places where a static diagram hides the thing that actually matters, which is how the output changes when an input changes.

How a chapter works

1. Understand

A short, plain-language explanation — with the derivation where the derivation matters.

2. Operate

Open the matching simulation and change one variable at a time.

3. Check

Answer a few questions; wrong answers are explained, not just marked.
