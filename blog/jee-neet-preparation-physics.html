JEE & NEET physics: a practical 6-month preparation plan

By R.M.VISANTHAN · 3 September 2026

Six months is enough time to prepare — but only if you use it deliberately. Here's a plan that works whether you're aiming for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or NEET.

Month 1–2: Build the foundation

Class 11 chapters first: kinematics, laws of motion, work-energy, rotation, gravitation, and fluids. These are the backbone that Class 12 electrodynamics and modern physics build upon. Study each concept until you can explain it to someone else — that's the test of understanding.

Month 3–4: Class 12 + problem solving

Electricity, magnetism, optics, and modern physics. As you finish each chapter, immediately solve problems. The gap between "I understood the chapter" and "I can solve the question" is where marks are won — close it with daily practice.

Month 5: Full syllabus revision + PYQs

Previous year questions (PYQs) are gold. They show you the exact style of questions, the difficulty, and the conceptual traps. Solve 10+ years of PYQs chapter-wise, then topic-wise. Mark every question you got wrong and revisit the concept behind it.

Month 6: Mock tests and accuracy

Switch to timed full-length mocks. The goal is accuracy under time pressure. After each mock, analyse: which questions were careless errors, which were concept gaps? Fix the careless errors first — they're the easiest marks to reclaim.

Daily routine that works

  • 2 hours: new concept study
  • 1 hour: problem solving on what you learned
  • 30 min: revision of formulas and derivations
  • Weekly: one full chapter test
