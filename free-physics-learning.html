There is no paid tier

There is no "pro" plan, no course to unlock and no feature held back. Nothing on the site asks for payment, and no lesson ends with an upsell. If a page is visible, its content is available in full.

Why it is free

The site is a static website with no servers to run for lessons and no video to stream. The simulations and the tutor compute in the browser, so serving another student costs almost nothing. That is what makes "free" sustainable here rather than a limited trial.

There is an optional support page if you want to help with hosting and new content. It is a choice, never a requirement, and nothing is gated behind it.

What the site deliberately does not do

  • No advertisements or sponsored placements
  • No account requirement or email capture
  • No tracking of your questions by the tutor
  • No selling of attention or data
