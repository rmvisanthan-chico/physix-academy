PhysiX Academy compared

Where PhysiX Academy fits next to PhET, Khan Academy and the big course apps — including the cases where they are the better choice.

These platforms solve different problems. PhET is a world-class simulation library, Khan Academy is a video course, and commercial apps sell structured coaching. PhysiX Academy is a chapter-by-chapter NCERT companion that puts a simulation, a short explanation and a worked-step tutor on the same page.

Where PhET is the better choice

If you want the widest possible physics simulation library, including chemistry and earth science, and you do not need chapter alignment, PhET is unmatched. Its simulations are research-based and translated into many languages.

Where Khan Academy is the better choice

If you learn best from long-form video explanations, or you need maths and other subjects alongside physics, Khan Academy's breadth is hard to beat.

Where PhysiX Academy is the better choice

  • You are studying the NCERT Class 9–12 sequence and want one page per chapter.
  • You want to operate the simulation, not watch a demonstration of it.
  • You want a numerical answer with the substitution shown, offline.
  • You do not want to create an account or sit through ads.

Honest limitation

PhysiX Academy has a smaller simulation library than PhET and no video library at all. It is maintained by one author, so new content arrives in batches rather than continuously.
