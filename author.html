Educator, author, and the creator of PhysiX Academy.

R.M. Visanthan is an educator and author working on physics education for Class 9–12 students. He created PhysiX Academy as a free alternative to ad-funded and paywalled study material: a single place where a student can read a chapter, operate the experiment, check the formula and verify a numerical answer.

What he wrote and built

  • The full NCERT Class 9–12 physics curriculum on the site — explanations, worked examples, common mistakes and revision notes
  • 40 interactive physics simulations and the 3D simulation set, built from the same equations the lessons teach
  • The offline AI physics tutor, a browser-based solver that shows worked steps without an external AI service
  • The formula library, calculators, practice question bank and adaptive quizzes
  • A printable Class 11 SL Arora study guide

Approach

The site follows one rule: every number shown on screen comes from the same relationship the lesson states. A simulation of a pendulum shows the period from T = 2π√(l/g), and the formula library, the calculator and the tutor use that same expression. This keeps the explanations, the animations and the answers from drifting apart.
