1. Physical World & Measurement — Dimensions & Errors

SL Arora habit: Check dimensions first. Significant figures = precision of least precise input. Errors: sum → add absolute, product → add relative. Example: s=ut+½at² passes [L] check.

2. Motion in a Straight Line — s–t, v–t, 3 equations

Formulas: v=u+at, s=ut+½at², v²=u²+2as. Example: Braking 20 m/s, a=−4 → s=50 m. Sim: kin1d

3. Motion in a Plane — Projectile

R=u²sin2θ/g, H=u²sin²θ/2g. 30° vs 60° same R. Sim: projectile.

4. Laws of Motion — Friction & Banking

f_s≤μ_sN, tanθ=v²/rg. Ex: 37° incline, μ=0.3 → slides. Sim: incline.

5. Work, Energy and Power — Work-energy & Springs

W=Fs cosθ, W_net=ΔKE, U=½kx². Ex: k=200, x=0.1 → U=1 J. Sim: energy.

6. System of Particles & Rotation — Torque, MI

τ=Iα, L=Iω, I=Σmr². Sphere vs ring: sphere rolls faster.

7. Gravitation — Kepler, Escape

g=GM/R², v_e=√2gR=11.2 km/s, T²∝r³. Ex: h=R/2 → g′=4g/9.

8. Mechanical Properties of Solids — Stress/Strain

Y=stress/strain, ΔL=FL/AY. Ex: Steel wire 2m, 1mm², 200N → ΔL=2mm.

9. Mechanical Properties of Fluids — Bernoulli

P+½ρv²+ρgh=const, A1v1=A2v2. Venturi P drop 6 kPa. Sim: buoyancy.

10. Thermal Properties of Matter — Expansion

Q=mcΔT, ΔL=αLΔT. Mix 0.2kg 80°C +0.3kg 20°C → 44°C.

11. Thermodynamics — First & Second Law

ΔU=Q−W, η=1−Tc/Th. Carnot 500K/300K → 40% max.

12. Kinetic Theory — Ideal Gas

PV=nRT, c_rms=√(3RT/M). O₂ at 300K → ~480 m/s.

13. Oscillations — SHM

T=2π√(m/k), T=2π√(L/g). 1m pendulum → 2.01 s. Sim: shm.

14. Waves — String & Sound

v=√(T/μ), f_n=nv/2L. Open pipe 1m → 170 Hz. Sim: wave.
