Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about what PhysiX Academy is, what it costs, and how it works.

Is PhysiX Academy free?

Yes. Every lesson, simulation, formula, calculator and practice question is free, with no ads, no paywall and no premium tier. There is nothing to buy and no account is required.

Does PhysiX Academy follow the NCERT syllabus for Class 9 to 12?

Yes. The curriculum follows the NCERT chapter sequence for Class 9, 10, 11 and 12 physics, and each chapter pairs an explanation with a matching interactive simulation and practice questions. See the NCERT coverage page.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up wall. Progress is stored locally in your browser, so the site works without an account and keeps working offline after the first visit.

Does PhysiX Academy have an AI physics tutor?

Yes. The tutor is an offline solver that runs in your browser and shows worked steps for topics such as Ohm's law, mirror and lens problems, kinematics, kinetic energy and echoes. It does not send your question to an external AI service. Read more on the tutor page.

Are there interactive physics simulations?

Yes. There are 40 interactive simulations covering mechanics, waves, optics, electricity, thermodynamics and modern physics, plus a 3D set with orbiting bodies, field lines and wave tanks. They run in the browser with nothing to install — see the simulation overview.

Does PhysiX Academy work offline?

Yes, after the first visit. The site is static and the tutor and simulations run locally, so lessons and simulations continue to work without an internet connection.

Does PhysiX Academy show advertisements?

No. There are no advertisements anywhere on the site and no sponsored content. The project is free and does not sell attention to fund itself. See why it is free.

Does PhysiX Academy work on a phone?

Yes. The lessons and simulations are built to work on mobile browsers as well as desktop, and the layout adapts to the screen you are using.

How is PhysiX Academy different from PhET or Khan Academy?

PhET is a general simulation library and Khan Academy is a broad video course. PhysiX Academy ties short lessons, simulations, formulas, calculators and practice directly to the NCERT Class 9-12 chapter sequence, with no video and no account. See the full comparison.

Who writes the content?

The content is written by R.M. Visanthan, an educator and author who created PhysiX Academy. It is original work inspired by standard textbooks including NCERT and SL Arora, not a verbatim copy.

How can I send feedback or report a mistake?

Email r.m.visanthan@gmail.com with the page or simulation name and what you found. Corrections to physics content are given priority.
