Seven self-contained pages, each one hand-plotted isometric bench. The flagship is the display-case: a glass cabinet on a plinth holding a ripple tank with its strobe lamp and a pendulum swinging at its true period T = 2π√(L/g) ≈ 1.68 s for a 0.70 m rod. Around it stand the ripple tank (crests and nodal lines from one water-wave model), the spring-damper (an envelope that follows the damping ratio), the circuit bench (brightness and power from Ohm's law), the lens bench (image position from the thin-lens equation), the pendulum (a dial that reads the period) and the projectile cannon (range, apex and time of flight from the ballistic model). Drag the knobs on any bench and every number on the page follows. Both dark and light themes are provided.
